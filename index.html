Part 2

RO Skids & PE Pipe‑to‑Pipe Heater

  • Piping and layout support for the RO/UF skids, from line routing through to QC sign‑off
  • Designed the "Pipe Element 500" — a stepped, concentric socket‑fusion heating die in SolidWorks — for joining PE pipe sections directly on site
  • One tool covers the full socket range in a single stepped element, from Ø12 mm up to Ø270 mm, sized to standard PE pipe diameters
  • Transmitted the project line list against the revised P&ID
Part 3

Pump Skid — Design, Vibration Analysis & Optimization

  • Designed pump supports for the RO/UF feed pumps in SolidWorks, stress‑checked to AISC
  • Ran a frequency (modal) analysis of the HP pump skid frame in SolidWorks Simulation to find natural frequencies and mode shapes
  • Identified two governing mode shapes — 57.6 Hz (frame torsion under the pump mounts) and 80.7 Hz (main beam bending) — and checked them against the pump's operating speed to avoid resonance
  • Optimized the frame's bracing based on the modal results to push natural frequencies clear of the excitation range
